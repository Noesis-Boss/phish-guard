Phish Guard

Warns you inside Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, and Proton Mail when a message may be pretending to be a brand.

Checks the open message locally. No attachments, inbox history, or email upload.

Mail warnings are off until you allow webmail access.

    Chrome asks for webmail access so Phish Guard can add warnings to the mail pages. It does not edit, delete, send, or report emails.
